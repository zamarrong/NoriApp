## Full description (1868/4000)

```
Nori is your company's field app: route sellers, counter staff, warehouse staff and supervisors work with the same data the office sees.

You need a Nori company account. There is no sign-up in the app: your administrator creates your user and gives you the company code, username and password.

ROUTE OF THE DAY
• Today's visits as a list and on a map, with each customer's time window.
• Check in and out, record sales, payments, and the reason when there's no sale.
• Open turn-by-turn directions to the next customer with one tap.

QUICK SALE
• Capture orders, deliveries, quotes and invoices from your phone.
• Free-sale mode for customers who aren't on your route.
• Find items by name or code, or scan the barcode.

PAYMENT AND TICKET
• Take payment with the methods your company uses. The app works out the change and asks for the transfer reference.
• Print the ticket on a Bluetooth thermal printer.
• Issue CFDI 4.0 invoices (Mexico). Your Nori server handles the stamping.

NO SIGNAL
• Keep selling and collecting without a signal.
• Everything you capture is saved on the phone and sent automatically when the connection is back, without duplicates.

CUSTOMERS AND COLLECTIONS
• Customer profile with balance, available credit and open documents.
• Apply payments to the invoices you choose.

PRICE CHECKER
• Scan a product to see its prices by price list and stock in each warehouse.

INVENTORY
• Warehouse transfers and physical counts from the floor.

SUPERVISION
• A dashboard with today's sales and collections, overdue receivables, and rankings by seller and by branch.
• Approve discounts or sales over the credit limit wherever you are.

You can also unlock the app with your fingerprint or face, and use it in dark mode. The app is in Spanish (Mexico).

Is your company not on Nori yet? Learn more at nori.software or write to hola@nori.software.
```
